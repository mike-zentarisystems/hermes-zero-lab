# Experimental Google Free Tier

Status: Documentation Verified only.

This is not the primary path. An eligible `e2-micro` may offer free-tier compute, but one GB of RAM is extremely tight, sustained CPU is limited, external IPv4 can cost money, IPv6-only egress may not reach every provider, and billing mistakes can create charges.

Before calling this route Lab Verified, test current terms, region, disk type, paid networking, memory, swap, provider IPv6 support, egress, Tailscale access, backup, restore, and billing controls.

Do not market it as guaranteed free until those tests are complete.
